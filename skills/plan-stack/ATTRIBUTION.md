Unslop. `to-plan` retains its own instructions, template, and upstream notices.

- Michael Denyer's pstack port, originally by Lauren Tan, informed the small
  entrypoint that resolves focused skills and owns the final response. The
  reference is `plugins/pstack/skills/poteto-mode/SKILL.md` at revision
  `88f6d39f228c4f9210d93bbf7e5e40a5c671fd5e`.
- DietrichGebert's Ponytail supplied the minimum-complete-solution ladder,
  omission of speculative work, preference for deletion, and safeguards. The
  pinned revision `f3da910b4f2827aacbb9762e32db14f915d72194` matches the skill
  supplied for this adaptation. Its persistence, intensity controls, and
  code-output rules are not part of this stack.
- Unslop is referenced from the same pinned pstack revision as an external
  writing skill. Its instructions are not bundled. Runtime behavior follows
  the installed version; the source pin records the version inspected here.
