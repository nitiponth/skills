# Attribution

`wait-what-th` adapts Matt Pocock's `wait-what` skill from
`mattpocock/skills` at revision
`5beb35b040e3de97e54fa9e386d7e42d15c295c2`, path
`skills/productivity/wait-what/`.

The adaptation preserves the explicit, one-shot re-pitch, the ability to back up
beyond the latest message, and the project's glossary conventions. It replaces
ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English with short, direct Thai while retaining
familiar business, technical, and project terms in English. It also preserves
executable and reference text such as code, commands, identifiers, paths, URLs,
and literal errors.

The upstream MIT notice is retained in [LICENSES.txt](LICENSES.txt). Original
contributions use the repository's MIT license. Exact source paths, pinned URLs,
and hashes are recorded in [sources.json](sources.json).
